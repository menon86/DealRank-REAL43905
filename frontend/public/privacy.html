DealRank privacy policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

DealRank is a student project (REAL 43905, Purdue Daniels School of Business) for comparing and ranking multifamily real estate deals. This page explains what information it handles.

What we collect

  • Your email address. When you sign in with Google or Microsoft, we receive your email address from the provider and use it to identify your account. The part before the @ is shown in the app as your username. We do not receive or store your password, and we do not use your Google or Microsoft account for anything else.
  • Deal data you enter. The deals, assumptions and numbers you add to the app are stored so you can come back to them.
  • Demo visits. "Try the demo" creates a temporary account with sample deals and no personal information. Demo data is deleted after 24 hours.

How it is used

Your email and deals are used only to run the app: signing you in and showing you your own deals. Each account sees only its own deals. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to build profiles.

The chat assistant

If you use the chat assistant, your questions and the details of the deals needed to answer them are sent to a third-party AI provider to generate a response. Do not enter information you would not want processed this way.

Where it is stored

The app runs on Render and its database is hosted on Neon. Data is stored with those providers in the United States.

Keeping and deleting your data

You can delete any deal in the app at any time. To have your account and all of its data removed, contact us using the details below and we will delete it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on the project's repository at github.com/menon86/DealRank-REAL43905.
