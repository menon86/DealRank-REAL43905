DealRank

Risk-adjusted multifamily deal comparison and ranking.

DealRank helps you underwrite multifamily acquisitions, compare them side by side, and see which one earns the most over your hurdle rate, adjusted for risk. It was built for REAL 43905 at the Purdue Daniels School of Business.

Open DealRank

What it does

  • Underwrite each deal with rents, expenses, financing and a full capital stack.
  • Compare NOI, cap rate, DSCR, cash-on-cash return, IRR and equity multiple.
  • Rank deals by return over your hurdle rate, with a risk-adjusted view.
  • Stress-test a deal with sensitivity sweeps and a Monte Carlo simulation.
  • Check a deal's rents against market data and export PDF or PowerPoint reports.

Signing in

You can sign in with Google or Microsoft. DealRank uses only your email address, to identify your account and show you your own deals. You can also try the app with sample deals and no sign-in. See the privacy policy for details.
